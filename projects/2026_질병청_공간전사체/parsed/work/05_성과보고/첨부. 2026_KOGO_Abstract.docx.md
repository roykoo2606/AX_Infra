AI-Driven Spatial Biomarker Discovery for Precision Medicine Innovation

Daeseung Lee

Portrai Inc., Seoul, Republic of Korea

Despite significant advances in genomic medicine, accurately predicting clinical outcomes and personalizing treatment strategies for complex malignancies—particularly ovarian, prostate, and gastric cancers—remains a critical challenge. This limitation often stems from the failure of conventional single-point biomarkers to capture the decisive information embedded in the spatial organization and cellular interactions within the tumor microenvironment. To address this, supported by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA), Portrai Inc. is conducting a specialized project to construct a large-scale, multi-modal dataset and develop Proof-of-Concept (PoC) algorithms. This innovative AI-based platform utilizes cutting-edge deep learning and quantitative pathology imaging to extract high-resolution Spatial Biomarkers from tumor tissue slides. The platform is designed to quantitatively map subtle spatial features—such as vasculature distribution, lymphocyte infiltration, extracellular matrix organization, and cellular heterogeneity—which are often invisible or subjectively assessed by traditional methods. We anticipate that the comprehensive datasets built through this initiative will demonstrate that spatial biomarker models achieve superior accuracy in predicting patient prognosis, recurrence risk, and immunotherapy response compared to existing markers. Specifically, distinct spatial patterns identified at the tumor-stroma interface are expected to serve as robust predictors of aggressive disease behavior. This project aims to establish a next-generation foundation for cancer diagnostics, leveraging AI to transform complex spatial biology into clinically actionable insights for precision oncology.
